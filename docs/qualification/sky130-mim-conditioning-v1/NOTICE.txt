SKY130 staged model and network fixture attribution

The fixture bundle contains source-derived SKY130 SPICE models and generated
qualification networks from the IC Design Studio SKY130 simulation package,
revision b26f7f28e659ddc1, package manifest SHA-256
840f7af802b07346650c50a5967e532a4cb1716623cf0c9d6fd6c58eaf35fcbe.
Public SkyWater model files retain their copyright and Apache-2.0 notices in
the files. IC Design Studio derivative adapters retain their GPL-3.0-or-later
notices. Both corresponding license texts accompany this bundle.

The fixture manifest and the staged pdk-model-files.json identify the included
model resources and their staged identities. Fixture provenance and the
separate validation record identify the retained source and physical evidence.
Packaged transformations are not represented as byte-identical upstream
files, and inclusion does not establish foundry qualification.
